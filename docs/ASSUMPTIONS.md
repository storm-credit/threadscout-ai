# Phase 1 Working Assumptions

These assumptions are derived from the project conversation and may be revised without rewriting the prototype architecture.

- Initial user: one Korean-speaking owner operating a personal Threads account primarily on mobile.
- Primary goals: reduce daily preparation time, grow the account, and test affiliate conversion.
- Initial audience: Korean parents and office workers who want practical, affordable products.
- Initial niches: value household products, elementary-school household items, travel/storage products, and low-risk food/snack discoveries.
- Daily operating target: twenty raw candidates, five recommendations, and up to three posts after human approval.
- Approval is required for every post.
- Products not personally used may be presented only as discoveries or candidates, never as first-hand reviews.
- Media must be directly created, licensed, or omitted.
- High-risk health, skincare, supplement, and unsupported child-development claims are excluded from automatic approval.
- Proposed posting slots for later scheduling work: 08:10, 12:20, and 20:30 Korea time.
- Phase 1 uses fixture data and local storage; it does not call external APIs.
