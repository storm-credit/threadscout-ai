# User Interview

Only unanswered decisions should be asked. Ask 3–5 questions at a time.

## Round 1 — purpose and user

1. Is this only for the owner's personal Threads account, or should it later support other users?
   - Recommended default: personal account first.
2. Is the primary goal account growth, affiliate revenue, operating-time reduction, or testing a future service?
   - Recommended default: validate growth and affiliate conversion while reducing daily preparation time.
3. Who is the intended reader?
   - Recommended default: Korean parents and office workers looking for practical, affordable products.
4. Which niche should be dominant for the first 30 days?
   - household/value products
   - child/elementary-school household items
   - office-worker tools and observations
   - food/snacks/travel purchases
5. What must the account never cover?

## Round 2 — content truth and media

1. Will products be purchased and photographed directly?
2. May the system recommend products not personally used, provided the post is clearly framed as discovery rather than experience?
3. Where will product photos and videos come from?
4. What evidence is sufficient to mark an exact product match?
5. What disclosure wording should be used for affiliate content?

## Round 3 — operations

1. How many draft candidates and published posts should be produced per day?
2. Is approval required for every post?
3. Which mobile actions are essential on the first screen?
4. What publication times should be supported initially?
5. Should a failed publish retry automatically, and if so, how many times?

## Round 4 — success measurement

1. What is the 30-day success threshold?
2. Which matters more: profile visits, saves, replies, link clicks, purchases, or revenue?
3. What amount of daily manual review time is acceptable?
4. What monthly tool/API budget is acceptable?
5. At what point should the project stop or change direction?
