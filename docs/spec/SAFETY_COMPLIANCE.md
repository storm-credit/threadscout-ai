# Safety, Rights, Privacy & Compliance Specification v1

This design establishes conservative product rules. Live operation still requires current platform/network/legal review before activation.

## 1. Truth classes

Every statement should be representable as one of:

- verified fact
- user first-hand experience
- opinion
- inference
- unknown

The Writer must not convert inference or unknown into fact.

## 2. Public figures

Allowed focus: public, product-relevant events with reliable sourcing.

Blocked:

- private-life rumors
- unverified relationships
- health/medical/mental-state speculation
- appearance or surgery speculation
- leaked/private material
- defamatory allegations framed as fact
- endorsement implication without evidence

A public figure's presence does not waive media rights.

## 3. Images/videos

- public availability ≠ permission to republish
- preserve creator/origin and rights state
- prefer owned/licensed media
- do not manipulate media so it changes factual meaning
- do not create fabricated imagery of a real public figure endorsing/using a product

## 4. Children/family products

Avoid developmental, educational, medical, safety, or therapeutic guarantees without strong appropriate evidence. Age/fit claims must come from reliable product information.

## 5. Health/skincare/supplement content

High-risk claims require a separately approved policy and evidence standard. Until then, the system should block disease treatment/prevention, guaranteed physiological effects, diagnosis, and similar claims.

## 6. Affiliate transparency

Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly. A commercial incentive never changes exact-match classification or Guardian severity.

## 7. Privacy/data minimization

Do not collect unnecessary personal information from comments or profiles. Store source excerpts/IDs only when needed for product evidence and audit. Do not ingest private-account content.

## 8. Platform integrity

No automated likes, follows, mass comments, unsolicited DMs, or engagement manipulation. Posting rate must respect platform capabilities and account trust.

## 9. Sensitive-event exclusions

Do not monetize tragedies, deaths, accidents, crimes, or crises merely because a related term trends. A product-relevant emergency/safety post requires separate policy review.

## 10. Guardian severity

### Block

- false exact-product claim
- rights violation
- rumor/private-life claim
- unsupported endorsement
- missing mandatory affiliate disclosure
- fake personal use
- serious health/safety overclaim

### Revise

- overstatement
- unclear attribution
- stale price phrasing
- weak limitation wording
- overly similar drafts

### Warning

- non-blocking freshness uncertainty
- low-value CTA
- minor style concern

## 11. Compliance versioning

Policy rules and disclosure text are versioned. A materially changed rule can invalidate scheduled content that relied on the prior version.
