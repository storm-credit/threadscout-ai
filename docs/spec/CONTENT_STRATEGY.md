# Content Strategy v1

## 1. Account promise

“유행만 따라가는 계정”이 아니라, 지금 관심받는 생활 아이템을 왜 뜨는지 확인하고 실제로 살 만한지 정리해 주는 계정.

## 2. Content portfolio

Working baseline:

- 50–60% useful/product discovery without aggressive affiliate CTA
- 20–30% verified product/affiliate content
- 15–20% comparison, failure, caution, or alternative content
- issue-triggered/celebrity-linked posts are a subset of the above, not a separate majority category

Actual ratios later adapt from validated metrics, subject to trust guardrails.

## 3. Four mandatory strategy angles

For every verified candidate, Strategist must produce four arguments, not four rewrites:

1. `problem_result` — what annoying problem it solves
2. `mechanism_demo` — why the mechanism is visually surprising/useful
3. `buying_decision` — what to compare/check before buying
4. `honest_fit` — who should skip it and who may benefit

Issue-linked candidates may replace one angle with `why_now`, but the remaining set must still include utility and honest limitation.

## 4. Hook rules

Good hooks expose useful tension:

- visible inconvenience
- surprising mechanism
- common buying mistake
- “looks good, but check this first”

Avoid:

- fake urgency
- invented scarcity
- unsupported superlatives
- shaming readers
- implying celebrity endorsement without proof

## 5. Trust markers

Useful trust signals include:

- exact product vs alternative label
- “직접 사용” only when true
- “찾아보니/확인해보니” for research-based content
- current price timestamp when price is mentioned
- a real limitation
- source/context attribution when necessary

## 6. Issue/public-figure content

The person/event is context, not the entire value proposition. Copy should answer:

- What product/feature are people asking about?
- What is actually confirmed?
- Is the exact item identified?
- What should the reader check before buying?

If the product value disappears without the person's fame, downgrade or reject the affiliate angle.

## 7. CTA taxonomy

- `save_for_later`
- `compare_before_buying`
- `check_exact_variant`
- `share_experience`
- `affiliate_link` only when mapping/disclosure valid

CTA must match evidence and not pretend urgency.

## 8. Duplicate prevention

The strategy layer should track repeated combinations of:

- hook logic
- problem statement
- product category
- CTA
- sentence pattern

High similarity should trigger a new angle, not thesaurus-style rewriting.
