# Daily Candidate Selection — Worked Example v1

Status: DESIGN ONLY.

The daily model narrows a synthetic pool of twenty candidates into a maximum of five recommendations. Opportunity score, evidence readiness, risk, freshness, duplication, and workload are evaluated separately. A high score never overrides a blocker. A lower-scoring ready candidate may be chosen before a higher-scoring unresolved candidate.

The system may end a day with fewer than three approved posts. Quality and evidence are more important than filling a quota.
